Sources

    Window chance. A member counts as a hit if any hour it has inside the window breaks the limit. Each source's chance is its share of hits; sources are weight-averaged and the result is rounded to the nearest 5%. A member only counts if it covers at least half of the window.

    Winds use the 10 m gust with runway 15/33 at 330° true: crosswind is gust × |sin(angle)| over 10 kt, head/tail is |gust × cos(angle)| over 15 kt. Precip within 30 nm is 1 km reflectivity of 20 dBZ or more anywhere within 30 nm for HRRR, HREF members and NAM, and 0.01 in/hr at any of 13 sample points out to 25 nm for NBM and the global ensembles. Lightning uses only models with explicit lightning output: the hi-res lightning field (HRRR and any member model that carries it) anywhere within 30 and 10 nm, and NBM's 1, 3 and 6 h thunderstorm probability, taking its highest value within each radius. Global ensembles and blends without a lightning parameter don't feed the lightning rows. Visibility under 200 ft uses NBM's probability of visibility below its lowest threshold at the site, plus the ECMWF HRES run judged against that same threshold (the threshold is listed under Sources). That threshold is well above 200 ft, so treat the value as an upper bound. NBM carries it only for the first few days, and HRES is used over the same hours so a single run never sets the value alone. High/Low are the weighted median of members' 11–18L maximum and 00–11L minimum.
